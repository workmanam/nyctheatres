# Theatres in Manhattan #
Interactive bivariate web map of musical theatres in Manhattan, New York City categorized on what type of theatre they are. Clicking on a point displays the name of the theatre. Using the search bar to look up the name of a theatre also locates the theatre's pin on the map.

### Theatre Types ###
* **Broadway Theatres:** Theatres located in New York's Midtown Theatre District with minimum seating capacity of 500 seats.
* **Off-Broadway Commercial Theatres:** Theatres with a capacity of less than 500 seats, whose profits benefit the theatre company.
* **Off-Broadway Non-Profit Theatres:** Theatres with a capacity of less than 500 seats, whose profits fund performers or theatre schools.

### Manhattan Districts ###
* **Manhattan Community Districts:** Subdivisions of Manhattan shaded based on the amount of theatres in each subdivision.
* **Broadway Route:** A shapefile of Broadway is overlayed on the map for spatial reference.

### Data Collection ###
* **Theatre Locations:** From *Theatre Pizzazz*.
* **NYC Districts:** From *NYC DCP Mapping Portal*.
* **Broadway Shapefile:** From *NYC DCP Mapping Portal*.
* **Search Bar:** From *MokahalA on Github*.
